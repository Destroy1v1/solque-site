FAQ

Questions.

How can I short a memecoin with no lending market?

You don't borrow the coin. Solque tracks its price and settles your profit or loss in SOL against the pool.

What happens if a coin rugs?

Shorts are closed at the last valid price and paid out. New longs on that coin are blocked.

When do I get liquidated?

When your losses reach your collateral. The exact liquidation price is shown before you open a position.

What are the fees?

0.1% to open and close. No funding fees on positions under 24 hours.
